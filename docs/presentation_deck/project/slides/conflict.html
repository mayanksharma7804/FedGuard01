Validation split, seed 42 (Findings F39). Strict: honest private clients are thrown out. Lenient: attackers get in.

This is Gap 1 of our paper, measured on real intrusion-detection training. DP noise pushes every honest update about 13 units away from the centre, while a sign-flip attack adds only about 1 to 1.5. A strict AutoGM collapses onto a single client - whose full noise then becomes the global update; a lenient one gives the attackers about a third of the weight. We chose lambda scale 4 as AutoGM at its best, so that every comparison is fair.
