HOSPITAL · MONDAY

BANK · THURSDAY

One new outbound connection

Same ransomware kit, same staging host.

Together they could name the campaign. Neither may share its network logs: they reveal patients, transactions and the internal network map.

The story from our paper's introduction. Each organisation sees a fragment; together they would recognise the campaign. But packet captures and flow logs are among the most sensitive records an organisation holds, and data-protection law forbids handing them over. So: how can organisations learn together without sharing data?
